Physics and the World Wide Web

Tim Berners Lee's Original Research

Tim Berners Lee's original research focused on the development of the World Wide Web, which was initially designed to facilitate the sharing of information among physicists at CERN. His work laid the foundation for the modern internet we have today, enabling researchers/users to access and share data more efficiently.

Current Uses of the World Wide Web

Today, the World Wide Web is used for a wide range of purposes beyond scientific research. It serves as a platform for social networking, e-commerce, entertainment, education, and much more. The web has become an integral part of daily life, connecting people and information across the globe.
